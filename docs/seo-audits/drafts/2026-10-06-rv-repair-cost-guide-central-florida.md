## Answer-first intro (58 words)
RV repair in Central Florida typically runs from a few hundred dollars for a roof reseal or AC service to several thousand for slide-out water damage or diesel chassis work. Nationwide Haul RV & Bus Repair in Lakeland gives a written estimate before any work starts, works with extended service plans and insurance claims, and handles both the chassis and the living space.

## How much does RV roof repair cost in Florida?
Industry ranges published by national sources put a full roof reseal at roughly $400 to $1,500 and a leak repair at $350 to $2,500, with full membrane replacement higher. Florida's UV and afternoon storms push roofs toward the top of those ranges when sealant is more than a year or two old. ## How much does RV slide-out repair cost?
| Repair | Typical range (national sources) | What drives it |
|---|---|---|
| Adjustment, lubrication, seal replacement | $150 to $500 | Labor only |
| Slide motor or controller replacement | $500 to $1,200 | Part cost, access |
| Mechanism or hydraulic overhaul | $1,000 to $5,000 | Full disassembly |
| Floor or wall water-damage repair | $2,000 and up | Rot extent |

## How much does RV AC repair cost?
Service and recharge $250 to $600; capacitor, fan or compressor work $600 to $1,800; rooftop unit replacement $1,000 to $5,000 depending on unit.

## What affects RV repair cost in Central Florida?
Checklist: parts availability (RV parts are not stocked like auto parts), chassis type (diesel pusher labor rates are higher), warranty or insurance paperwork, snowbird-season demand (Sept to Mar), and whether water damage has spread.
